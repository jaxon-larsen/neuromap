Also called a quantum NOT gate, because it's the quantum equivalent of the classical NOT gate
Swaps the amplitudes of [[Computational Basis State]]s
- $X|0\rangle = |1\rangle$
- $X|1\rangle = |0\rangle$
Represented as a 2 x 2 matrix:
$X = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$
Represents a $180\degree$ rotation about the $x$-axis on a [[Bloch Sphere]]
States lying along the $x$-axis (like $|+\rangle$ and $|-\rangle$) remain on the $x$-axis
