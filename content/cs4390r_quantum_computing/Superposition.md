Nondeterministic
Definition of a single [[Qubit]] state in superposition:
$$|\phi\rangle=\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle$$
- **$\vert{}\phi\rangle$ (State Vector):** Represents the quantum state of a single qubit using [[Dirac Notation]].
- **$\vert{}0\rangle$ and $\vert{}1\rangle$ (Computational Basis):** The standard [[Orthonormal]] basis vectors corresponding to classical binary values $0$ and $1$:
    $$\vert{}0\rangle = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \vert{}1\rangle = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$
- **$\alpha$ and $\beta$ (Probability Amplitudes):** Complex numbers ($\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{C}$) weighting each basis state.
