Represented by $H$
The primary tool used to put a qubit into a superposition of both $0$ and $1$ simultaneously.
$$H = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix} $$
Maps standard [[Computational Basis State]]s (the Z-basis, $\{|0\rangle, |1\rangle\}$) to the superposition states (the X-basis, $\{|+\rangle, |-\rangle\}$)
