Values $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are probability amplitudes of the [[State Vector]] $\begin{bmatrix} \alpha \\ \beta \end{bmatrix}$
$\alpha$ tells you "how much $|0\rangle$" is in the state
$\beta$ tells you "how much $|1\rangle$" is in the state
They must be [[Normalized]] so |$\alpha|^2 + |\beta|^2 = 1 \quad$
