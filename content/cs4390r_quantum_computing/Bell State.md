Also known as EPR pairs
These states have [[Maximum Entanglement]]
- $\vert{}\Phi^+\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\vert{}00\rangle + \vert{}11\rangle)$
- $\vert{}\Phi^-\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\vert{}00\rangle - \vert{}11\rangle)$
- $\vert{}\Psi^+\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\vert{}01\rangle + \vert{}10\rangle)$
- $\vert{}\Psi^-\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\vert{}01\rangle - \vert{}10\rangle)$
