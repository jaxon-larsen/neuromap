$\text{logit}(p) = \ln\left(\frac{p}{1 - p}\right)$
The ratio $\frac{p}{1-p}$ represents the odds of the event occurring
Allows us to model the relationship between predictor variables and a binary outcome linearly by stretching the $\{0,1\}$ probability range to the [[Log-odds Scale]] $\{-\infty,+\infty\}$
Function is opposite of a [[Logistic Curve]]
Assumes error terms follow a logistic distribution
