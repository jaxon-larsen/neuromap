Algorithm used for classification
Predicts the probability of a categorical dependent variable with two possible outcomes
We model a binary outcome $Y \in \{0, 1\}$ conditioned on covariates $X$. We want to estimate $p = P(Y = 1 \mid X)$
Uses the [[Logistic Curve]] to map inputs to probabilities between 0 and 1
Allows you to find the probability model with maximum entropy
