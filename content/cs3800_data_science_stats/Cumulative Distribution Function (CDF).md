$$F_X(x) = P(X \le x)$$
Gives the probability that a random variable $X$ takes a value less than or equal to a specific number $x$
For instance, when flipping two coins, where $X$ is the number of heads, $F_X(1.4)=.75$ because the probability of flipping less than 1.4 heads is .75

## Inverse CDF or Quantile Function
$$F^{-1} (q) = \text{inf}\{x:F(x) > q\}$$
